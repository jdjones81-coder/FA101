Step-by-step guides

Click each section for a detailed breakdown. These are sample workflows that you will fill with your exact wording and legal/regulatory references.

MPN — Master Promissory Note [toggle]

  1. Gather ID: SSN, DOB, Contact info, and school details.
  2. Login: Use the federal student aid (FSA) ID at studentaid.gov.
  3. Complete borrower info: Review personal and loan terms carefully.
  4. Review repayment: Read sample repayment schedules and options.
  5. Sign: Electronically sign with your FSA ID and save a PDF copy.

Tip: always print/save the signed MPN and the confirmation number.

Entrance Counseling (EC) [toggle]

  1. Access EC: Go to studentaid.gov and sign in with your FSA ID.
  2. Provide school & loan info: Ensure correct school and estimated loan amounts are selected.
  3. Interactive modules: Review budgeting, repayment expectations, and responsibilities.
  4. Assessment: Complete required short quizzes to confirm understanding.
  5. Complete: Receive confirmation and note any follow-up items from your financial aid office.

FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid [toggle]

  1. Create/Use FSA ID: Applicants and (if dependent) parents each need an FSA ID.
  2. Gather documents: Tax returns, W-2s, bank statements, and records of assets.
  3. Complete sections: Student info, school list, dependency status, financials.
  4. Sign & submit: Sign with FSA ID; save confirmation and SAR/ISIR when available.
  5. Follow up: Respond promptly to verification requests from schools.
